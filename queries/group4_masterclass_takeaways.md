# Top 10 Takeaways — Content Masterclass 2026

**Source:** [Content Masterclass: Sådan får du kreativ succes på sociale medier i 2026](https://webinar.obsidian.dk/content-masterclass-sadan-far-du-1) (Obsidian, 3-hour webinar)
**Transcript:** `transcriptions/group4/content_masterclass_sådan_får_du_kreativ_succes_på_sociale_medier_i_2026.txt`

The things to change and do right for creative success on social media:

**1. Win the first 1–3 seconds with a real hook**
You have only 1–3 seconds — the hook is the *dørmand* (bouncer) for the whole video. Build from the six hook types as templates (curiosity, problem-solving, emotion, reframe, negativity, specific confession — e.g. *"Jeg lavede video i et år, før jeg forstod hvad jeg gjorde forkert"*), but always add personality so it sounds human. **Don't** open with *"Hej, jeg hedder…"* — it wastes ~3 seconds unless you're Tom Cruise.

**2. Buy a mic and an SSD before a camera**
There's no right/wrong format — an iPhone story often beats a cinema shoot — so good audio is the highest-ROI purchase: it makes even a 2004 camcorder read as a "creative choice." Røde Wireless Pro is ~3,500 kr, but the cheaper Røde/DJI "Go" mics (~1,500–2,000 kr) are plenty. Then a Samsung T7 SSD (1 TB min, 2 TB for 4K) and edit in DaVinci Resolve (free tier is excellent) or CapCut.

**3. Shoot wide, batch, and capture multiple hooks/lengths**
Frame loose so the same footage crops to 9×16, 1×1, and 4×5 (16:9 only for YouTube/TV). Batch into one focused 8-hour shoot and deliberately film several hooks, openings, and lengths of the *same* content so it can be A/B tested and the winner emerges. Pull SFX/music from Motion Array, Epidemic Sound, or Artlist.io.

**4. Stimulate multiple senses; change something every 2–3 seconds**
Multisensory hooks (visual + audio + text) make messages stick — combine a physical hook in front of the camera with a post-edit crash-zoom and a sound effect. After the hook, keep something new happening every 2–3 seconds (new angle, B-roll, graphic) or the brain disengages. Always add subtitles (they create *fremdrift*, serve no-sound viewers, trigger "phantom sounds"); skipping music should be a deliberate choice.

**5. Script everything — and use the *papegøje-metoden***
The best "natural" videos are never shot from the hip; without structure you ramble 2–3 minutes when the video needs 15–20 seconds. On set, someone behind the camera says each line aloud and the talent repeats it (*papegøje*/parrot method) — this kills *øh/ehm* filler and dead eyes. Treat the script as a *pejlemærke*, not a cage.

**6. Use AI as a starting point, not a final product — brief it heavily**
AI (they prefer Claude for scripts) is *"ikke en genvej til god content, men en genvej til mere content"* — gold in, gold out. Give it a system instruction as a critical sparring partner (stored in a `CLAUDE.md`/project), feed it your brand, target groups, tone, and pain points, and end prompts with *"give me a summary of how you understand this task."* Then edit by hand. Ban AI tells: *"I en verden hvor…"*, *"ikke poste mere, poste smartere"*, *"næste niveau."*

**7. With AI content, own it 100% or skip it — never fake reality**
Land in one camp, never the middle: either fully AI and openly labeled (the Kræftens Bekæmpelse SAR campaign showed AI-aged skin *as* obvious AI and was praised), or raw authentic UGC ("Jabber" style — handheld, unscripted). The "AI in Disguise" study (369M impressions) found AI ads perform fine *unless* consumers suspect they're AI — hidden AI is what kills you.

**8. Maximize entity-ID diversity — content is ~70% of Meta performance**
Meta says content drives ~70% of performance and groups near-identical ads under one *entity-ID*, refusing to properly test overlapping creatives. Aim for a high diversity score (their own campaign hit **82%** — 10 ads = 10 entities, not 5) using genuinely different formats: static, AI animation, talking-head EGC, Minecraft voiceover, Reddit "social debate," song, etc. (Entity-IDs apply to paid Meta only — not organic or LinkedIn.)

**9. Master the 8 components first, then a framework, then scale 3–4 formats**
Formats are just the scaling layer; the engine is 8 components: core desire, (unique) mechanism, avatar/persona, angle, authority, valence + intensity, positioning, awareness stage. Wrap them in a framework (Hook–Pain–Agitate–Reframe–Mechanism–Trust–CTA, or PAS/AIDA), then scale a winner across 3–4 fitting formats — don't run all 15–16. Flow: pick components → hypothesis → test in formats → scale winners; when one fatigues, start over with new components.

**10. Build for the full funnel — stop only talking to the bottom 3%**
Most advertisers only address the buy-ready few (only ~3% of B2C / ~5% of B2B), hit a ceiling, and waste the other 95%+. Map content to the 5 awareness stages (unaware → most-aware), weighting effort toward the top: educational/documentary for unaware, pain-point hooks + UGC/podcast for problem-aware, comparison/EGC for solution-aware, testimonials for product-aware, offer/urgency for most-aware. Build *psychographic* personas (not demographic boxes), and prefer steady always-on content over short bursts: *"lidt content der kører hele tiden slår meget content der kører i kortere perioder."*
